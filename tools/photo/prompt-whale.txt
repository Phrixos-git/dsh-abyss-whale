Underwater wildlife photograph taken at night, extreme realism.
A single adult humpback whale in full side profile, horizontal, swimming to the right, the entire animal from the tip of the rounded rostrum to both fluke tips inside the frame, with a clear margin of open water around it.
The whale is lit only by bioluminescent plankton suspended in the water: a pale cyan-white rim light runs along the dorsal ridge, the small falcate dorsal fin, the long pectoral flipper and the serrated trailing edge of the flukes, and thousands of tiny blue-white sparks cover the skin, densest along the back and the throat.

Camera: 50mm lens, f/2.8, sharp focus on the animal, gentle underwater haze, faint volumetric glow around the body, no sun, no surface, no reef, no diver, no other animals, no bubbles in front of the lens, no text, no watermark, no border.
